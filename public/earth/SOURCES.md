# Earth sky catalogue

Downloaded 2026-10-29 from https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial:

- `stars.6.json`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial/master/data/stars.6.json
- `constellations.lines.json`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial/master/data/constellations.lines.json
- License: `LICENSE-d3-celestial` (BSD-3-Clause, Olaf Frohn).

Upstream credits the star catalogue to **XHIP: An Extended Hipparcos Compilation**, Anderson E. and Francis C. (2012), VizieR V/137D: https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/V/137D

Constellation figures are from the IAU constellation page with modifications by Olaf Frohn. Coordinates use equatorial longitude/right ascension in degrees and declination, in the J2000 frame. The data files are included unchanged.

Sun/Moon ephemerides and coordinate rotations: **Astronomy Engine**, Don Cross, MIT, https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy (npm package `astronomy-engine` 2.1.19).
